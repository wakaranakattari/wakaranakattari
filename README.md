## my philosophy
> for me, programming has never been about standard solutions or sitting with the same comfortable tool forever. it's a canvas for creativity. i believe that stepping outside the mainstream and exploring the "strange" corners of tech shapes how we think-it makes us versatile, innovative, and open-minded. 
> 
> as an infj, i naturally want to share this feeling with others. i love expanding people’s horizons, showing them that the world of code is much wider and more beautiful than they think. if a language is considered "dead" or too niche, it usually just means people haven't truly understood its soul yet.
